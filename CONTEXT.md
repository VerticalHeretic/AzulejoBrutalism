# Azulejo Brutalism

A colour theme, inspired by Brutalism and Portuguese azulejo tilework, shipped as a Neovim colorscheme plus matching themes for other apps.

## Language

**Variant**:
One complete colour assignment of the theme: Light, Dark or OLED.
_Avoid_: mode, flavour, style

**OLED**:
The Variant that is Dark with a true-black background and surfaces sunk toward black; a peer of Light and Dark, used in place of Dark.
_Avoid_: black mode, AMOLED

**Extra**:
The theme ported to an app other than Neovim (Ghostty, Zed, Obsidian, …).
_Avoid_: port, integration, app theme
